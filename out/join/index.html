Learn. Practice. Build. Compete.

WHO CAN JOIN?

  • All SMVDU students (any year, any branch)
  • No prior coding experience required
  • Just curiosity and willingness to learn
  • Open to students from other colleges for select events

WHY JOIN?

Improve Problem-Solving

Sharpen your algorithmic thinking through consistent practice and guided sessions.

Compete & Win

Participate in coding contests, hackathons, and debugging competitions with prizes.

Learn Practical Tech

Workshops on Git, Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, cloud, and modern development tools.

Build Real Projects

Collaborate on web apps, mobile apps, open source, and hackathon projects.

Explore Open Source

Guided first contributions, contribution drives, and maintainer mentorship.

Connect & Grow

Network with peers, seniors, alumni, and industry mentors who share your interests.

Questions?

Is there a membership fee?

No. Code Club is completely free for all students.

Do I need to know coding?

Not at all. We welcome beginners and help you learn from scratch.

What is the time commitment?

Flexible. Attend events that fit your schedule — no mandatory attendance.

Can non-CSE students join?

Absolutely. We have members from ECE, IT, ME, and other branches.
